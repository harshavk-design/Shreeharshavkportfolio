Introduction

Designing with purpose, thinking beyond screens.

I'm a UI/UX and Product Designer passionate about understanding people, identifying meaningful problems, and creating digital products that make everyday experiences simpler.


With over six years of experience in design, I bring a strong foundation in visual design and interface development, alongside a growing focus on UX research, product thinking, usability, and accessibility.


My approach combines creativity with structured problem-solving. I aim to understand the why behind every design decision, validate assumptions with users, and create experiences that are both useful and easy to use.
